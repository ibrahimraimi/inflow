Welcome to Inflow Analytics! This guide will walk you through the initial steps to get your analytics project up and running.

## Why Inflow?

Inflow is designed for developers and business owners who value:
- **Privacy**: No cookies, no personal data collection, fully GDPR/CCPA compliant.
- **Performance**: Lightweight tracking script (< 1KB) that doesn't slow down your site.
- **Ownership**: Self-hosted or managed, you own your data.
- **Real-time Insights**: See your traffic as it happens.

## System Requirements

Inflow is a web-based platform. To use the dashboard, we recommend the latest version of:
- Google Chrome
- Mozilla Firefox
- Microsoft Edge
- Safari

## Account Creation

1. **Sign Up**: Navigate to the [Signup page](/signup) and create an account using your email or Google.
2. **Email Verification**: Once registered, check your inbox for a verification email. Click the link to activate your account.
3. **Join or Create an Organization**: Inflow uses a multi-tenant structure. You can create your own organization or accept an invitation to join an existing one.

## First-time Setup

After signing in, you'll be prompted to set up your first organization:

1. Enter your **Organization Name**.
2. Upload a **Logo** (optional).
3. Click "Create Organization".

## Adding Your First Website

Once your organization is ready, follow these steps to start tracking:

1. Click the **"Add Website"** button on the dashboard.
2. Enter the **Website Name** (e.g., My Portfolio).
3. Provide the **Domain** (e.g., `example.com`).
4. Select your **Timezone** to ensure accurate reporting.
5. Click **"Add Website"**.

> [!TIP]
> You can enable "Localhost Tracking" in the settings if you want to test the implementation during development.
