This page outlines common tasks you might want to perform in Inflow and how to achieve them efficiently.

## Workflow 1: Setting up Analytics for a New Marketing Campaign

If you're launching a new campaign, you'll want to track its performance specifically.

1. **Ensure UTMs are used**: When sharing your link, append UTM parameters (e.g., `?utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=launch`).
2. **Visit Inflow Dashboard**: As traffic comes in, go to your website's dashboard.
3. **Filter by Campaign**: Look for the "Campaigns" section (can be found in Sources or by specific UTM filters) to see how that specific traffic is performing.

## Workflow 2: Identifying "Dead" Pages

Find pages that users are leaving frequently.

1. Go to the **Pages** report.
2. Toggle the view to **Exit**.
3. Sort by the highest number of exits to see which pages are the last ones users visit before leaving.

## Workflow 3: Sharing a Report with a Client

*Note: Public dashboards are currently in the roadmap. For now, use this workflow:*

1. **Invite as Member**: Invite your client to the organization with the "Member" role.
2. **Direct to Dashboard**: Provide them with the URL to the specific website's dashboard.
