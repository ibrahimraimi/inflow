Inflow is built for teams. You can organize your websites into **Organizations** and invite collaborators with specific roles.

## Organizations

Everything in Inflow belongs to an organization. When you first sign up, you create your own organization, but you can be a part of multiple organizations.

### Creating an Organization
1. Click on your profile/organization name in the sidebar.
2. Select **"Create Organization"**.
3. Choose a name and optional logo.

## Manage Team Members

Collaborate with your colleagues by inviting them to your organization.

### Inviting Members
1. Go to **Settings** > **Team**.
2. Enter the **Email Address** of the person you want to invite.
3. Select a **Role** (see below).
4. Click **"Send Invitation"**.

They will receive an email with a link to join your organization.

## User Roles

Inflow uses Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):

| Role | Permissions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Owner** | Full access to everything, including billing and deleting the organization. |
| **Admin** | Can manage websites, view analytics, and invite/remove members. |
| **Member** | Can view analytics and website settings but cannot manage the team. |

## Managing Invites

You can view the status of sent invitations in the **Invites** tab. You can revoke pending invitations at any time.
