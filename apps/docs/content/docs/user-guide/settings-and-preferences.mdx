---
title: Settings & Preferences
description: Configure your account, security, and organization preferences.
---

Customize Inflow to fit your needs. Access settings by clicking your profile icon in the sidebar.

## Account Settings

- **Profile**: Update your name and profile picture.
- **Email**: Manage your primary email address.
- **Password**: Change or reset your password.

## Organization Settings

- **General**: Change the organization name or upload a new logo.
- **Teams**: Manage members and invitations.
- **Billing (Pro)**: Manage your subscription and view invoices.

## Website Settings

Each website has its own configuration:
- **Name & Domain**: Update the friendly name or registered domain.
- **Timezone**: Set the reporting timezone for that specific site.
- **Localhost Tracking**: Toggle whether traffic from local development environments should be recorded.

## Security

- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: Add an extra layer of security to your account (available in Account Settings).
- **Active Sessions**: View and manage devices currently logged into your account.
