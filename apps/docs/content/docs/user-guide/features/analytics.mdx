The Inflow dashboard provides a real-time overview of your website traffic. It's designed to give you the most important data at a glance.

![Inflow Dashboard](/images/guide/4.png)
*Typical analytics dashboard view in Inflow.*

## Core Metrics

At the top of your dashboard, you'll see four key metrics:

- **Visitors**: The number of unique individuals who visited your site. We use a privacy-respecting hash of the IP address and User-Agent to identify unique visitors without storing PII.
- **Visits (Sessions)**: A visit is a group of interactions by a single user within a specific timeframe (usually 30 minutes).
- **Views**: The total number of pages viewed by all visitors.
- **Bounce Rate**: The percentage of visitors who left your site after viewing only one page.
- **Visit Duration**: The average time a visitor spent on your site.

## Real-time Traffic

The line chart displays your traffic trends over the selected time range. You can toggle between **Visitors** and **Views** to see different perspectives of your growth.

## Detailed Reports

Below the main chart, you'll find detailed breakdowns of your traffic:

### Pages
See which paths on your website are the most popular. You can view:
- **Path**: The URL path.
- **Views**: Total number of views for that page.
- **Entry**: Where users started their journey.
- **Exit**: Where users left your site.

### Sources
Understand where your traffic is coming from:
- **Referrers**: Domains that link to your site (e.g., `twitter.com`, `google.com`).
- **Channels**: Grouped traffic types (Direct, Social, Organic Search).

### Locations
A geographic breakdown of your visitors by **Country**, **Region**, and **City**. We also provide a **Map View** for a global visual representation.

### Environment
See what technology your visitors are using:
- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.
- **OS**: Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, etc.
- **Device**: Mobile, Desktop, Tablet.

## Filtering Data

Use the filters at the top of the dashboard to:
- **Change Date Range**: Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, or a Custom Range.
- **Filter by Property**: Click on any item in the tables (e.g., a specific country or browser) to filter the entire dashboard by that property.
